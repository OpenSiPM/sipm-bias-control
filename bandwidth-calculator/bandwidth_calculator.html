SiPM Low Pass Filter Calculator Rev 1
OpenSiPM 2026-10-06

Picks the analog bandwidth for the SiPM transimpedance amplifier low pass filter so that it matches the optical resolution and scan speed of a laser scanning microscope [1][3]. A cutoff that is too high adds noise (lower sensitivity); one that is too low blurs the image (lower resolution).
